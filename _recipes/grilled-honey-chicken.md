---
layout: recipe
title: "Grilled Honey Lemon Chicken"
ingredients:
  - "4 boneless skinless chicken breasts, pounded to an even thickness"
  - "⅓ cup vegetable oil"
  - "Juice of 2 lemons (about 6 tablespoons)"
  - "4 tablespoons honey"
  - "1 teaspoon garlic powder"
  - "1 teaspoon salt"
  - "1 teaspoon Italian seasoning"
  - "¼ teaspoon black pepper"
instructions:
  - "Whisk together the oil, lemon juice, honey, garlic powder, salt, Italian seasoning, and black pepper."
  - "Place the chicken in a large resealable bag. Add half of the sauce, seal the bag, and refrigerate for at least 1 hour or overnight. Reserve the remaining sauce."
  - "Preheat the grill to medium heat. Remove the chicken from the bag, discard the used marinade, and grill for 5–7 minutes per side, until cooked through."
  - "While the chicken cooks (or immediately afterward), pour the reserved sauce into a small saucepan and bring it to a boil over medium-high heat. Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer for 5–6 minutes."
  - "Drizzle the cooked sauce over the grilled chicken and serve immediately. Garnish with lemon wedges and fresh herbs, such as basil, if desired."
servings: 4
