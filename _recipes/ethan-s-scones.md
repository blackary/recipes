title: "Ethan's Scones"
photo: assets/images/recipes/ethan-scones.webp
ingredients:
  - "1/3 cup butter"
  - "1 3/4 cups flour"
  - "3 tablespoons sugar"
  - "2 1/2 teaspoons baking powder"
  - "1/2 teaspoon salt (easy on salt)"
  - "1 egg, beaten"
  - "1/2 cup craisins"
  - "1/2 cup white chocolate chips"
  - "4-6 tablespoons half-and-half"
  - "1 egg white"
instructions:
  - "Heat oven to 400°F."
  - "Cut butter into flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt with a pastry blender until mixture resembles fine crumbs."
  - "Stir in the beaten egg, craisins, white chocolate chips, and just enough half-and-half so the dough leaves the side of the bowl."
  - "Turn dough onto a lightly floured surface."
  - "Knead lightly 10 times."
  - "Roll or pat 1/2 inch thick."
  - "Place on an ungreased cookie sheet."
  - "Brush dough with egg white (Ethan doesn't)."
  - "Bake until golden brown, 10-12 minutes."
servings: 6-8
---
