---
layout: recipe
title: "New York-Style Cheesecake"
ingredients:
  - "1½ cups graham cracker crumbs (from about 12 whole crackers)"
  - "5 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted"
  - "2 tablespoons sugar"
  - "⅛ teaspoon salt"
  - "32 ounces cream cheese (four 8-ounce blocks), at room temperature"
  - "2 cups sugar"
  - "3 tablespoons all-purpose flour"
  - "4 teaspoons vanilla extract"
  - "1 teaspoon packed lemon zest (from 1 lemon)"
  - "2 teaspoons fresh lemon juice (from 1 lemon)"
  - "¼ teaspoon salt"
  - "6 large eggs"
  - "½ cup sour cream"
instructions:
  - "Set an oven rack in the lower-middle position and heat the oven to 375°F. Wrap the bottom and sides of a 9- or 10-inch springform pan in two seamless layers of extra-wide heavy-duty foil, then coat the inside with nonstick spray."
  - "Mix the graham cracker crumbs, melted butter, 2 tablespoons sugar, and ⅛ teaspoon salt. Press the mixture firmly and evenly over the bottom of the pan."
  - "Bake the crust for 10 minutes, then remove it from the oven. Reduce the oven temperature to 325°F and bring a kettle of water to a boil."
  - "Beat the cream cheese, 2 cups sugar, and flour on medium speed until just smooth, about 1 minute. Scrape the bowl well."
  - "On low speed, mix in the vanilla, lemon zest, lemon juice, and ¼ teaspoon salt. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing only until each disappears into the batter. Mix in the sour cream just until uniform; avoid overmixing."
  - "Place the springform pan in a large roasting pan and pour the filling over the crust. Add enough boiling water to the roasting pan to reach about 1 inch up the outside of the springform pan."
  - "Bake at 325°F until the edges are set and the center has a slight wobble, 1 hour 30 minutes to 1 hour 45 minutes. If the top browns too quickly, tent it loosely with foil."
  - "Cool the cheesecake in the water bath on a wire rack for about 45 minutes, until the water is warm rather than hot."
  - "Remove the springform pan from the water bath and discard the foil. If needed, run a thin knife around the edge. Cover and refrigerate for at least 8 hours and up to 2 days."
  - "Remove the pan sides and slice with a sharp knife, cleaning the blade between cuts. Serve plain or with berry sauce."
servings: "8 to 10"
---

Adapted from [Once Upon a Chef's New York-style cheesecake](https://www.onceuponachef.com/recipes/new-york-style-cheesecake.html) by Jennifer Segal.

Use a 9- or 10-inch springform pan and extra-wide heavy-duty foil so the water bath cannot seep through seams. The cheesecake can be frozen for up to 3 months; thaw it overnight in the refrigerator.
